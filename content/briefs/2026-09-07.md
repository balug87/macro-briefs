## Cascades

War → chokepoints → crude/diesel → headline CPI and inflation expectations → FOMC/ECB/BoJ path → front-end yields and the dollar → freight and food inflation with a lag. Equities can still rally a session on an “in-line headline.” That is not the cascade.

## Outlook — base case + key risks

### 1 month
**Base:** 25 bp Fed hike on 16 Sep; BoJ hikes; oil stays three digits while the Strait is contested; risk assets chop after the Friday bounce.
**Risks:** A hold after 80%+ odds (front-end rally, dollar dip, Warsh credibility hit). A closed Hormuz for more than a news cycle (diesel and Europe gas, not just Brent).

### 6 months
**Base:** Shallow G10 tightening finishes; oil war premium fades only if a ceasefire or a secure corridor appears; core inflation does not make a clean run at 2%. Growth slows from the energy tax, not from a 2020-style collapse.
**Risks:** Second-round inflation in services if diesel and food stick. Europe winter gas if storage and LNG routing stay ugly. A policy error either way — too slow on headline, too fast on a weakening labor market.

### 2 years
**Base:** The 2026 oil/war shock is remembered as a mid-cycle supply break that kept policy higher for longer than the 2025 narrative wanted. Capex keeps gravitating to energy, grids, and secured minerals. AI infra remains power-constrained, not idea-constrained.
**Risks:** A real recession if tightening + oil run together. A frozen conflict that institutionalizes $80–110 oil as the “normal” band and rewires trade around the Cape.

### 5 years
**Base:** Chokepoint politics and mineral security are standing features of the planet picture, not a 2026 episode. Demographics and public debt cap how far any one central bank can run a 1970s replay. Mining and refining capacity, not just ore in the ground, set the resource constraint.
**Risks:** A wider great-power war that takes the dashboard off “monitor” and onto something else. A clean peace + productivity burst that collapses the war premium and leaves this brief looking hawkish.
